What this data cannot tell you

No tract has an observed unbanked rate

The FDIC survey stops at state and large metro. Tract layers show conditions that correlate with being unbanked, not account ownership.

Tract estimates are noisy

Most ACS tract shares have a coefficient of variation above 40%. Hatched tracts should not be ranked against each other.

HMDA is mortgages only

It shows credit access for home loans, not deposit accounts. Protected-class fields were not used and should stay out of any eligibility logic.

CFPB complaints are not a sample

Counts reflect who chose to complain and which companies are large locally. Use issue mix, not volume, as the signal.
